About Us

Veyana School of Style is created with a vision to make fashion education more practical, professional and meaningful.

Our approach combines fashion theory with practical learning so that students understand not only what to create, but why and how it is created.

From garment construction and professional draping to portfolio development and presentation, students are guided through a complete learning journey.

Veyana is not simply about learning fashion. It is about discovering your design identity and building the confidence to express it.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

01 — VISION

Our Vision

To build a fashion education ecosystem where students develop professional knowledge, practical skills, creative confidence and a strong design identity that can connect them to opportunities beyond the classroom.

02 — MISSION

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide structured fashion education that combines theory, practical training, professional techniques, portfolio development and career-oriented learning. We aim to help every student understand their potential and turn their fashion skills into meaningful professional opportunities.
